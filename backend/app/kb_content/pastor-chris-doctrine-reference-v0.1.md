# Pastor Chris Oyakhilome — Doctrinal Terminology & Teaching Reference

## 0. Purpose and how HAN should use this document

This document gives HAN a working understanding of how Pastor Chris Oyakhilome uses and defines the key terms of his teaching, so HAN can help TNI translators render his books, teachings and the daily devotional *Rhapsody of Realities* accurately.

It is a **reference for meaning**, not a translation memory. It contains **no target-language terms**. HAN must never invent a target-language equivalent for a doctrinal term. Where a term has no approved equivalent in the project glossary, HAN should say so and ask the translator (consistent with the TNI rule *"Do not invent terminology, context or client instructions"*).

**Operating rules for HAN when this document is retrieved**

1. **Human authority.** Everything HAN offers is a suggestion for a human translator to review. Doctrinal wording is approved by people, never by HAN.
2. **Explain meaning, then ask.** When a translator asks "what does *X* mean here?", answer from Section 4 and cite the Rhapsody date/title. When asked "how do I translate *X*?", first check approved terminology/TM for that language; use this document only to explain what the term must *preserve*.
3. **Flag, don't smooth.** If a source sentence makes a strong or distinctive claim (for example, that righteousness does not increase, or that believers are "partakers of the divine nature"), HAN must not soften, harmonise or "correct" it in translation. Flag any target-language wording that would change the claim.
4. **Scripture is not re-translated.** Quoted Bible verses should use the approved Bible version for the target language (see Section 3.4), not a fresh machine translation.
5. **Say when evidence is thin.** If a term is not covered here (see Section 7), HAN must say it has no reference on file instead of improvising a definition.
6. **Never reproduce the source texts.** This document is a synthesis. HAN should not output long passages of *Rhapsody of Realities*; the original is copyrighted by LoveWorld Publishing.

## 2. The core theological frame (how the teaching hangs together)

Understanding this frame helps HAN predict meaning when a sentence is ambiguous. Across the six months the teaching repeatedly makes the same moves:

1. **Substitution and exchange.** Jesus took the believer's place (sin, judgment, separation from God) so the believer could take His place (righteousness, life, sonship). Phrases: *divine substitution*, *the price was fully paid*, *the sinless substitute*.
2. **New Birth as a change of nature, not a change of religion.** The believer is born of God's incorruptible seed (the Word) and receives God's life and nature. Christianity is described as *Christ in you*, not as a religion.
3. **Gift, not performance.** Righteousness, acceptance and standing before God are *received*, not earned or maintained by effort. Right living flows *from* righteousness.
4. **Union with God.** The believer is "joined to the Lord" and "one spirit" with Him; the Holy Spirit lives in, and is mingled with, the believer's spirit.
5. **Consciousness.** The believer is urged to live in conscious awareness of what is already true in Christ (righteousness-consciousness, victory-consciousness, consciousness of the indwelling Spirit).
6. **Position and authority.** The believer is seated with Christ "far above all principality and power"; therefore dominion and victory are the normal, daily life.
7. **The Word as life and environment.** God's Word carries God's life; meditation on it renews the mind and sustains the believer.
8. **Fellowship with the Father, Son and Holy Spirit** is the goal of redemption.

## 3. Voice, style and format conventions of *Rhapsody of Realities*

### 3.1 Structure of each daily article (preserve in translation)

1. Day and date line; title in capitals.
2. **Theme scripture** in a shaded box (with reference).
3. Body of roughly 5–8 short paragraphs, opening with a drop-cap.
4. A **PRAYER** *or* a **CONFESSION** (bold), ending "…in Jesus' Name. Amen." (prayers) or often "Hallelujah!" (confessions).
5. **FURTHER STUDY** — a list of scripture references (some marked with a version, e.g. AMPC, NIV, MSG).
6. **1-YEAR BIBLE READING PLAN** and **2-YEAR BIBLE READING PLAN** — chapter references only.
7. Occasional footer boxes (contact details, app subscription, testimony website, salvation prayer pages). These are region-specific; HAN should flag them for the translator to confirm per language/region rather than translate blindly.

### 3.2 Tone

- Direct address ("you"), declarative and pastoral. Strong, confident assertions ("You're not an ordinary person").
- Frequent contractions in English ("you're", "isn't", "He's"); the register is conversational-authoritative, not academic.
- Short exclamations of praise ("Hallelujah!", "Blessed be God!", "Glory to God!", "What a glorious Gospel!"). These are sincere devotional expressions; preserve their warmth and brevity.
- Occasional first-person anecdotes by the author (for example, personal illustrations in the July and October articles). Keep first person; do not neutralise.
- Illustrations from everyday life (a president's office, a manufacturer's manual, DNA, fish and water, a seed). Translate the *image*; flag if an image does not travel culturally and ask the translator.

### 3.3 Typographic and capitalisation conventions (English source)

- Pronouns for God (He, His, Him) and **Name** (as in "Jesus' Name") are capitalised; so are **Word** (God's Word), **Gospel**, **Church**, **Kingdom**, **New Birth**, **Spirit/Holy Spirit**, **Godhead**, **Cross**. HAN should flag target-language rendering that loses these distinctions where the target language has a way to mark them (capitalisation, honorific, or conventional Bible-translation practice).
- British spelling is used in the 2026 edition (recognise, honour, favour, fulfil, authorise).
- Direct Bible quotations are bold-italic in the body; verse reference follows in parentheses.

### 3.4 Bible versions

The edition's default is the **King James Version**. Other versions are abbreviated in the front matter (NKJV, AMP, AMPC, TANT, TLB, CEV, NASB, ISV, NIV, MSG, WEB, NLT, ASV, TEV, RSV, GNB, WNT, NRSV, MOFFATT, WESNT, EBR, DRB, TPT, CJB, DNT). Translation guidance for HAN:

- Where a verse is quoted from KJV, the translator should normally use the approved Bible in the target language.
- Where the author quotes a **non-KJV version** (for example, AMPC with its bracketed expansions, or MSG's "perpetual victory parade") and builds the argument on that exact wording, the translator must be warned that substituting the target-language Bible may break the point being made. HAN should flag this and ask the translator how to proceed.

### A. God, the Spirit and the Godhead

#### 4.1 Holy Spirit

**Meaning.** The Holy Spirit is a **Person and God Himself**, not an influence, force, feeling or "junior" God. He is "the Spirit of the Father", one with the Father and the Son. Every miracle Jesus did was done by the Father (the Holy Spirit) dwelling in Him. The believer *receives* the Spirit at the New Birth and is then taught to *recognise* Him — to respond to Him as the divine Person who lives within. The Spirit speaks "in line with the Word of God", guides, teaches, reveals the Father's will, and is described as "God's final solution" for human limitation.

Several linked teachings recur:

- **Indwelling, not external.** The power of God is *within* the believer; the New Testament believer does not wait for power to "come down". (May 6)
- **Anointing = the Holy Spirit in the believer.** There is no separate "second" or "double" anointing to chase; the believer already has "all there could be", and the need is to activate what is already received. (May 2, May 9)
- **Mingled / one spirit.** The Spirit did not come to stay *beside* the believer's spirit but to be *united* with it — "one spirit", not two. (June 24, Sept 21)
- **Continual filling.** "Be filled with the Spirit" (Eph. 5:18) is explained from the Greek as continuous and ongoing, a way of life rather than an occasional experience; being "led" (Rom. 8:14) is explained from Greek *agō*: lead, guide, conduct, carry along, impel or induce. (Sept 12, Nov 17)
- **Poured out on all flesh.** Joel 2:28 is fulfilled at Pentecost; the Spirit works in people everywhere to make them receptive to the Gospel. (Sept 9)
- **Fellowship ("communion").** See 4.16.

**Key scriptures.** John 14:16-17, 16:13; Rom. 8:11, 8:14; 1 Cor. 6:17; Eph. 5:18; 1 John 2:20, 2:27; Acts 1:8; Joel 2:28; 2 Cor. 3:17; 2 Cor. 13:14.

**Translation notes.** Preserve personhood: use the personal pronoun the target language uses for a divine person, and avoid wording that reads as "a force", "energy" or "influence". Keep "Holy Spirit" and "Spirit of God" consistent with the project's approved scripture terminology. Distinguish the Holy Spirit (capitalised) from the human spirit (lower case) — the teaching depends on this contrast. Do not render "induced by the Spirit" (Nov 17 title) in a way that suggests coercion or manipulation; the point is being governed, directed and motivated by the Spirit.

**Do not confuse with.** "Anointing" as a special upgrade; "power" as a sensation (see 4.18); a "second blessing".

**Sources.** Nov 3 *The Holy Spirit Is Not 'Junior' God*; Nov 5 *Recognise the Person of the Holy Spirit*; Nov 6; Nov 17; May 2; May 6; May 9; June 24; Sept 9; Sept 11; Sept 12; Sept 21.

#### 4.2 God is a Spirit / the Godhead

**Meaning.** "God is a Spirit" describes God's *nature*, not an impersonal force. God is a **Person** with personality, purpose, intelligence and will. The Greek *pneuma* ("spirit") is also used for wind; the author says the wind picture illustrates some characteristics (felt, unseen) but does not define God's nature. The Lord Jesus is "the express image of the Father's person" and the complete revelation of what the Father is like. The Godhead (Father, Son, Holy Spirit) is the object of the believer's fellowship.

**Key scriptures.** John 4:24, 14:9; Heb. 1:3, 12:9; Col. 1:15; 1 Cor. 1:9; 1 John 1:3.

**Translation notes.** "Spirit" must not be translated with a word meaning only "ghost", "wind" or "breath" if the target language has a distinct theological term; follow the approved Bible in the target language. "Godhead" appears only a few times in these editions; HAN should ask for the approved term rather than inventing one.

**Sources.** Nov 10 *God Is a Spirit*; Nov 29 *Fellowship with the Spirit*; May 13–14 (titles on Christ's deity and Lordship).

### B. The Word and Truth

#### 4.3 The Word of God

**Meaning.** God's Word is more than information or inspiration:

- **It carries God's life.** "Everything that comes out of God carries His life", as DNA carries a person's identity, even from a strand of hair. Hence "the words I speak … are spirit and … life" (John 6:63). (Nov 8)
- **It is seed.** The Lord Jesus was born, humanly, of the incorruptible seed of God's Word; believers are likewise born again "of incorruptible seed, by the word of God" (1 Pet. 1:23). (Nov 4, Nov 27, Sept 27)
- **It is food, environment and manual.** The Word nourishes the believer's spirit like daily manna (June 3); it is the believer's "true life and environment" (Sept 27); it is the manufacturer's "instruction manual" for the life God designed (Oct 28).
- **It must be meditated on and lived.** Meditation (Josh. 1:8) renews the mind until the Word becomes the believer's mentality (Nov 25); the extent to which one knows the Word determines the extent to which one can manifest Christ's life (Sept 20).
- **"The Word" is also a title of Jesus** (the living Word, John 1:1, 14), not only the Bible.

**Key scriptures.** John 1:1, 1:14, 6:63; Isa. 55:11; Heb. 4:12; 1 Pet. 1:23; James 1:18; Josh. 1:8; Col. 3:16; 2 Tim. 3:16-17.

**Translation notes.** Capitalise "Word" when it means God's Word or Christ. Preserve the seed/life/DNA imagery; do not reduce "carries His life" to "is powerful". The seed image is carried in the approved Bible's rendering of "seed"/"incorruptible" — check the glossary.

**Sources.** Nov 8 *God's Word Carries His Life*; Nov 4; Nov 25; June 3 *Continually Nourished by the Word*; Sept 20 *Live the Word to the Fullest*; Sept 27 *The Word—Your True Life and Environment*; Oct 28 *Consult the Manual*.

#### 4.4 Truth

**Meaning.** In these editions "truth" has three connected senses:

1. **Moral integrity / honesty.** Truth is a gift one gives; it "stands on its own", needs no defending, and does not depend on being believed. Liars strain to be believed; those who speak truth are at peace. (May 1)
2. **God's Word.** "Thy word is truth" (John 17:17); grace and truth are *God's* grace and *God's* truth. (July 26)
3. **The revelation of God's reality in Christ** — "who He is, what He accomplished and who we have become in Him." (Oct 12) The Holy Spirit is "the Spirit of truth" who guides into all truth.

**Key scriptures.** John 1:17, 8:32, 14:17, 16:13, 17:17; Eph. 4:25.

**Translation notes.** Keep "grace and truth" as a paired expression exactly as in John 1:17. Where the target language separates "truth" (factual) from "truthfulness/faithfulness", ask the translator which sense the paragraph needs.

**Sources.** May 1 *The Character of Truth*; July 26 *Grace and Truth Are Revealed in Him*; Oct 12 *Grace and Truth Came by Jesus Christ*.

### C. Salvation doctrine

#### 4.5 Righteousness

**Meaning (central, most frequent teaching).** Righteousness is **a gift from God, not a human achievement**. Specifically:

- It is **God's nature of rightness** — His ability to be "only and always right"; His equity and fairness in judgment. It belongs to God; no one can attain it by effort. (June 2)
- Human righteousness, however moral, is inadequate before God ("filthy rags", Isa. 64:6); the righteousness that counts is "of God", revealed apart from the Law. (June 1, Oct 11)
- It is **received, imparted, bestowed** at the New Birth — "you don't try to be righteous; you receive it." The author's preferred verbs are *imparted* and *given*. (June 1, June 2, Oct 22)
- It makes the believer **as righteous as Jesus**, because it *is* His righteousness; therefore it is **perfect, complete and cannot increase or decrease**. The author lists areas of real growth (faith, knowledge of the Word, wisdom, expression of the divine life) and then says explicitly that righteousness is not one of them. (Oct 25, May 7)
- It is distinct from **right living**: righteousness is the *nature*; right living is its *expression*. Right living is the result, not the means. (Oct 22)
- It produces **boldness before God** — no guilt, condemnation or inferiority; "righteousness-consciousness". Maturity is linked to understanding it ("unskilful in the word of righteousness", Heb. 5:13). (June 2, Oct 16)
- It is received **by faith**, through the Gospel — "righteousness by faith". (July 21, July 20)

**Key scriptures.** Rom. 1:16-17, 3:21-26, 5:1, 5:17, 10:1-4; 2 Cor. 5:21; Phil. 3:7-9; Isa. 54:17, 64:6; Heb. 5:13; 1 Cor. 6:11.

**Translation notes.** 
- Keep **righteousness / justification / right standing** as a family of terms. In many languages these share one root; if the target language has only one word for "justice/righteousness", flag it, because the teaching depends on "righteousness of God" being God's own nature given as a gift, not social justice or moral conduct.
- Do **not** translate with a word that means only "good behaviour" or "being just/fair" (law-court or social sense) unless the approved Bible uses it.
- Preserve the verb choice: *imparted / given / bestowed / received* — **not** "earned", "attained" or "acquired".
- Preserve the denial that righteousness "grows"; flag any translation that implies progress ("more righteous").
- Keep "righteousness" vs "right living" as two different terms.

**Do not confuse with.** Moral uprightness; holiness as conduct; sanctification as a process; "imputed" (the author uses "imparted").

**Sources.** June 1 *Understanding True Righteousness*; June 2 *The Gift of Righteousness and Its Power*; July 21 *Righteousness by Faith*; Sept 26 *The Consciousness of Your Righteousness*; Oct 11 *Righteousness Apart from the Law*; Oct 16 *Stand Firm in Righteousness*; Oct 17 *The Legal Side of Our Righteousness*; Oct 22 *Right Living Begins with Righteousness*; Oct 25 *You Don't Grow in Righteousness*; May 7 *Grace and Righteousness*.

#### 4.6 Justification

**Meaning.** To be justified is to be **declared righteous / acquitted / declared not guilty** — a legal pronouncement by God Himself, "the legal side of our righteousness". It is received freely by grace through the redemption in Christ. Jesus was "delivered for our offences and raised again for our justification" (Rom. 4:25): His resurrection is God's declaration that justice was satisfied. Result: **peace with God**, no condemnation, and refusal of self-recrimination ("self-denunciation" is not humility).

**Key scriptures.** Rom. 3:24-26, 4:24-25, 5:1, 8:1; Acts 13:38-39; 1 Cor. 6:11.

**Translation notes.** Keep the court-room sense (declared, verdict, acquittal). "Justification" and "righteousness" are linked but not identical: justification is the legal declaration; righteousness is the gift/nature.

**Sources.** Sept 30 *Justification by Faith*; Oct 17; Sept 26; Nov 7 *The Price Was Fully Paid*.

#### 4.7 Salvation

**Meaning.** Salvation is more than rescue from hell: it is deliverance from **spiritual death (separation from God)** into union and fellowship with Him; it brings healing, deliverance and establishment in righteousness. Psalm 27:1's "the LORD is … my salvation" is read as: the believer carries the *phenomenon* of salvation — the solution before any problem. The Gospel is the **power of God unto salvation**.

**Key scriptures.** Rom. 1:16; Ps. 27:1; Heb. 7:25; John 3:16; Isa. 59:2.

**Translation notes.** Salvation is not only future; keep present-tense, experiential wording where the source uses it. "Spiritual death" = separation from God, not cessation of existence — flag renderings that imply annihilation.

**Sources.** July 22 *The Phenomenon of Salvation*; Nov 30 *No Longer Separated from God*; July 2 *Don't Lose Your Salvation* (title only; not reviewed in detail).

#### 4.8 The Gospel

**Meaning.** The Gospel has "a definite content, a definite power and a definite purpose": it is **the power of God unto salvation** (Rom. 1:16) and the place where **the righteousness of God is revealed**. The power is "in the message itself", not in human persuasion. It is "the revelation of divine substitution": Jesus took the believer's place so the believer could take His. It must be *understood*, not merely announced — Paul preached it even to Christians.

**Key scriptures.** Rom. 1:16-17; 2 Cor. 5:21, 8:9; 1 Cor. 2:4; Acts 13:38-39.

**Translation notes.** Capitalise "Gospel". Keep "good news" connotation only if the approved Bible does; do not drop the power/revelation sense.

**Sources.** July 20 *What the Gospel Truly Is*; June 20 *The Power Is in the Gospel*; Sept 19 *Revelation of Divine Substitution*; Sept 28 (title: *Know the Gospel More Than Anything Else*).

#### 4.9 Redemption (and substitution)

**Meaning.** **Substitution:** the sinless Jesus — born of the Word, not of a human father's seed — took humanity's place, bore sin, judgment and separation, and died the death. "Every one of us was on that Cross"; the price was fully paid; no further punishment or sacrifice remains. **Redemption:** the author draws a careful distinction — redemption *from the curse of the Law* applied to those under the Law (the Jews); the Gentiles were never under it. He then says Christians were **"reborn" rather than "redeemed"** in the sense of the Law's curse, and stresses the "great difference".

**Key scriptures.** Isa. 53:6; Heb. 9:11-14, 10:4-14; Gal. 3:13-14; Eph. 2:12; 2 Cor. 5:21; 1 Pet. 1:18-19.

**Translation notes.** The Oct 18 distinction is nuanced and likely to be mistranslated by harmonising it with common usage ("redeemed" for all believers). HAN should **flag** this article for human doctrinal review before any translation is finalised and never "fix" the wording.

**Sources.** Nov 4 *The Sinless Substitute*; Nov 7 *The Price Was Fully Paid*; Nov 27; Sept 19; Oct 18 *Redemption from the Curse of the Law*; Oct 20 (title: *Christ Jesus—The Ultimate Redeemer*).

### D. New life and identity

#### 4.10 New Birth / Born Again / New Creation / New Creature

**Meaning.** At the New Birth the Holy Spirit **immerses** the believer into Christ (1 Cor. 12:13 — "not water baptism") and the believer is **born of God** — receives God's life and nature. The person becomes a **new creation / new creature** (2 Cor. 5:17): old things passed away; the natural life is "supplanted" by the divine life. Related emphases:

- **"A new class of men … a new species"** with "the God-life" — eternal life is *this* specific life in the Son, the very life of God. (July 9)
- **Traced to Christ, not ancestry.** The believer is not defined by family lineage, ethnicity, nationality or inherited weakness; Adam (the first man) vs Christ (the last Adam) as two heads. (Nov 26, Oct 30)
- **God's workmanship** — "what God has wrought". (Nov 21)
- **Born into God / union with Christ.** (Nov 2, Sept 21)
- **Three groups from God's view:** the Jews, the Gentiles, and the Church — "the new creation". (Oct 30)

**Key scriptures.** 2 Cor. 5:17; 1 Cor. 12:13, 15:45; 1 Pet. 1:23; James 1:18; Eph. 2:10; Rom. 5:12, 5:17; Col. 3:9-11; 2 Pet. 1:4; 1 John 5:11-13.

**Translation notes.** "New creation" and "new creature" translate the same Greek idea (*kainē ktisis*) in 2 Cor. 5:17; follow the approved Bible and keep the term consistent across articles. "Born again" must stay distinct from "reformed", "converted to a religion" or "joined a church" — July 9 explicitly says it is "not about joining a church".

**Sources.** Nov 1–2, 21, 26; July 9 *A New Class of Men with a Distinct Life*; July 6 *There's Something About You*; Oct 30; Sept 16 (title: *Born of the Word and the Spirit*).

#### 4.11 Divine nature, "gods", sonship and the "God-kind"

**Meaning.** Using 2 Peter 1:4, the author explains "partakers of the divine nature" from the Greek *koinōnoi theias physeōs* as **"associates or companions of the God-kind"** — as a human child is fully human before maturing, the born-again believer is fully "of God's kind". Psalm 82:6 ("ye are gods") is cited; he writes that this describes the **life** received, and clarifies that this "doesn't mean you suddenly know everything God knows experientially". Believers are sons of God (1 John 3:2), heirs and joint-heirs with Christ (Rom. 8:17).

**Translation notes — HIGH SENSITIVITY.** These phrases are easily misread as claiming equality with God or as polytheism. HAN must **flag** such passages for the doctrinal reviewer, must not soften or intensify them, and must preserve the author's own qualifications. Where possible, rely on the approved Bible rendering for Ps. 82:6 and 2 Pet. 1:4.

**Sources.** Sept 21 *Unveiling of a Divine Union*; July 5 *Knowledge of Your True Nature*; July 9; Nov 28 *Beyond Religion*.

#### 4.12 Spirit, soul and body; the human being

**Meaning.** The human being is **a spirit, has a soul, and lives in a body** (Nov 9). The Old Testament temple is used as a model of the person: **outer court = body; holy place = soul; Most Holy Place = spirit** — the believer's spirit is where God dwells (July 15). The **soul** (mind, will, emotions) must be taught, trained and governed by the Word and brought under the influence of the Spirit (July 16; Rom. 12:2). The body is to be brought under subjection (July 17, title). Spiritual death is separation of the spirit from God; the physical body is the "house" in which the spirit lives (Nov 30).

**Translation notes.** Keep the three-part terminology consistent; many languages have overlapping words for "spirit/soul/heart". Ask the translator for the approved anthropological terms and do not merge them. "Natural man" vs "spiritual man" (1 Cor. 2:14-15) is a technical contrast in Nov 9 and Nov 19.

**Sources.** July 15 *Your Spirit Is the Most Holy Place*; July 16 *Train Your Soul to Follow Your Spirit*; Nov 9; Nov 19; Nov 30.

### E. Grace, mercy, love, kindness

#### 4.13 Grace

**Meaning.** Grace is **divine favour — God's gracious influence upon a person's life to see that he succeeds** (the Hebrew *chen*, "gracious favour"). In Christ it is God's loving disposition toward man expressed in salvation, "not because we deserved it". Features taught in these editions:

- **Works from within, according to calling.** Grace is given "according to God's purpose for our lives" and may bless those connected to it by God's plan. (May 7)
- **Influences mind, heart and decisions** beyond natural ability, covers, shields and protects. (May 29)
- **Can increase** ("grace … multiplied", 2 Pet. 1:2; "he giveth more grace", James 4:6) — unlike righteousness. The Greek *lambano* ("take hold and make it yours") is used: believers can receive as much grace as they take hold of; don't limit it. (Oct 24)
- **All-promoting.** Grace "pilots" the believer to reign in life — upward and forward only; grace reigns through righteousness (Rom. 5:20-21). (July 27)
- **Grace and truth came by Jesus Christ**, in contrast to the Law given through Moses. (July 26, Oct 12)
- **Approach the "throne of grace" boldly** to obtain mercy and find grace to help. (Heb. 4:16; Oct 1)

**Translation notes.** Keep **grace** distinct from **mercy** and **favour**; the author uses all three but with separate roles. "Grace" is not "pardon" only. Preserve "all-promoting" (promotes/elevates). The contrast *righteousness cannot grow, grace can increase* (May 7, Oct 24–25) must survive translation.

**Sources.** May 7; May 29 *Grace, Mercy, and Peace*; July 26; July 27 *His Amazing and All-Promoting Grace*; Oct 1; Oct 12; Oct 24 *Don't Limit God's Grace*; June 10 (title: *Take Advantage of His Grace*).

#### 4.14 Mercy (and covenant kindness)

**Meaning.** In the New Testament believers have **already obtained mercy** (1 Pet. 2:10); they do not cry out for it or try to persuade God to be merciful. The Old Testament word *hesed* is explained as **"covenant kindness" — kindness rooted in covenant, not in merit**, flowing "from the greater to the lesser". The model is David and Mephibosheth (2 Sam. 9): "Fear not" is "always the language of mercy", the one with nothing is brought to the king's table, and David calls it **"the kindness of God"**. Grace and mercy work together: when grace locates a man, mercy follows (Noah). 

**Translation notes.** Do not translate *hesed* only as "pity". Keep "mercy" distinct from "grace", and keep "kindness of God" as the author's specific phrase. "Mercy … obtained" is past/completed, not a continuing request — preserve the tense.

**Sources.** Oct 1 *You've Obtained Mercy*; May 29; May 31 *The Kindness of God*.

#### 4.15 Love, benevolence, compassion

**Love.** Jesus is **"the true definition of God's love"** — love tabernacled in human flesh; human definitions of love do not decide what God's love is, even though *agape* was a known Greek word. (May 12) Believers are "the Father's love-child" (Oct 1).

**Benevolence.** God's **benefaction** — His constant disposition and readiness to do the believer good; one does not have to qualify for it. (July 30)

**Compassion.** The word **"compassion" does not occur in any of the six editions provided.** HAN must **not** infer a definition. The nearest concepts are *mercy* (hesed, 4.14), *kindness of God*, *benevolence* and *love*. ### F. Wisdom, knowledge, consciousness

#### 4.16 Wisdom

**Meaning.** **Christ is made unto the believer wisdom** (1 Cor. 1:30), so divine wisdom is at work in the believer's spirit, giving discernment, insight and the right response in every circumstance — "never confused, perturbed or overwhelmed". The believer "judges all things" (1 Cor. 2:15) from God's perspective; "you're not only someone who has solutions; you're a solution" (Nov 25). Wisdom can **grow** (unlike righteousness — Oct 25). God's wisdom looks to realities that last forever; human reasoning is preoccupied with immediate outcomes (Oct 7). Grace helps the mind think right thoughts (May 29). The Word is to dwell richly "in all wisdom" (Col. 3:16).

**Translation notes.** Keep wisdom (divine, spiritual) distinct from "intelligence", "cleverness" or "natural reasoning" — the source often contrasts them. Where the source says "wisdom of Christ in your spirit", keep it as an indwelling reality rather than a skill one acquires.

**Sources.** Nov 19 *The Spiritual Man Judges All Things*; Nov 25 *We Don't Fail*; Oct 7 *Think Beyond This World*; May 29.

#### 4.17 Knowledge

**Meaning.** Three kinds are emphasised:

1. **Knowing God.** Faith's strength rests on knowing the God one believes — "the people that do know their God shall be strong" (Dan. 11:32); keep "increasing in the knowledge of God" (Col. 1:10; 2 Pet. 3:18). (Nov 16)
2. **Knowing who you are in Christ** — knowing, not guessing, that one has eternal life now (1 John 5:13). (July 5)
3. **Knowing the Word** — one can only live the truths one knows. (Sept 20)

**Translation notes.** "Know" here is experiential and relational (to know a person), not only informational. HAN should ask the translator to check that the chosen word is not purely "to have information about".

**Sources.** Nov 16 *Your Knowledge of Him*; July 5 *Knowledge of Your True Nature*; Sept 20; June 3.

#### 4.18 Consciousness ("-consciousness")

**Meaning.** A signature word: **conscious awareness of a truth already settled in Christ** that shapes thinking and behaviour — *righteousness-consciousness*, *consciousness of His indwelling presence*, *victory-consciousness*, *spiritual consciousness*, "consciousness of your heavenly position". It is the opposite of sin-consciousness, guilt, inferiority. Consciousness is **cultivated** through the Word, meditation and fellowship with the Spirit. Count: ≈116 occurrences.

**Translation notes — IMPORTANT.** "Consciousness" is **not** "conscience" (moral sense of right/wrong). Many languages use one word for both; HAN must ask the translator to select a term meaning *awareness/mindfulness of a reality*.

**Sources.** Nov 1, 9, 20; Sept 26; Oct 16; May 2.

### G. Faith and practice

#### 4.19 Faith

**Meaning.** "The expectation of the fulfilment of God's Word and acting accordingly" (May 8). Its foundation is **God's Word, not feelings or experiences** — one should not make doctrine from personal sensations. The strength of faith is rooted in knowing God (Nov 16; Dan. 11:32). **"Giving glory to God"** is the attitude of strong faith (Abraham, Rom. 4:20-21), expressed through **declarations, offerings and daily conduct** (Nov 11). God deals with people on the basis of faith, which brings them into righteousness (July 21). Faith "doesn't recognise impossibilities" because it knows the One it believes (Nov 15).

**Translation notes.** Do not translate as "belief" in a purely intellectual sense where the source uses faith as confident action. Keep "by faith, not by sight" distinct from "by feelings".

**Sources.** May 8 *The Right Foundation for Faith*; Nov 11 *The Attitude of Strong Faith*; Nov 15; Nov 16; July 21.

#### 4.20 Offering vs donation

**Meaning.** An **offering** is **holy**: an act of worship and a **testimony of faith before God** that He is source and provider; Abraham, Isaac and Jacob worshipped with offerings. A **donation** is gathered for a specific purpose (meeting a need, a project, charity). The author insists the two are different and that an offering should be presented consciously, reverently and joyfully.

**Translation notes.** Keep two different words where the target language allows; flag if the language only has one word for "gift/donation/offering". Avoid fundraising language.

**Sources.** Nov 12 *The Sacredness of Your Offering*; Nov 11.

#### 4.21 Fellowship (koinōnia), partnership, distribution

**Meaning.** The Greek *koinōnia* (2 Cor. 13:14, "communion") carries **three connected meanings: fellowship, partnership and distribution** — "fellowship gives birth to partnership, and partnership gives birth to distribution." Fellowship with the Holy Spirit is more than speaking in tongues; it means recognising and walking with Him as a person. Partnership brings His influence into home, job, finances and health; distribution is the grace, wisdom, power and blessings flowing through the believer to the world (Genesis 17:1-2 and 12:2 as the pattern). Fellowship with the Father and the Son is enjoyed *through* the Holy Spirit (Nov 29). Christianity is "a call to fellowship with the Godhead" (1 Cor. 1:9; 1 John 1:3). Fellowship is described as "our natural way of life" (Nov 6).

**Translation notes.** The three-part sense is a key teaching; HAN should warn if the chosen target word for "communion/fellowship" loses "partnership" or "distribution". Do not render as a ritual only ("Holy Communion") unless the passage is about Holy Communion (see 4.26).

**Sources.** Nov 23 *Fellowship, Partnership and Distribution*; Nov 29; Nov 6; Nov 28; July 8 (title: *The Fellowship That Changes Everything*).

#### 4.22 Glory; "shouting 'Glory!'"

**Meaning.** "Glory" is used both as the **manifest majesty of God** and as a **Spirit-prompted shout** of response to His presence and sovereign power (Ps. 29:9). Believers "manifest God's glory"; they are God's glory and He theirs (Nov 22). "Glory to God!" is also a frequent exclamation.

**Translation notes.** Keep the shout "Glory!" as a short, bold exclamation. Preserve the distinction between the noun "glory" and the exclamation.

**Sources.** Nov 13 *When We Shout, "Glory!"*; Nov 22; passim.

### H. Church and Kingdom

#### 4.23 The Church

**Meaning.** The Church is **the people of God, not a building** (July 13). In the early Church there were many house fellowships yet "one body". The Church is **Christ's Body and "the fullness of Him that filleth all in all"** — "we are His completeness" (Eph. 1:22-23; Nov 22). The Church is built on **Christ**, the "Petra" (rock/foundation), not on Peter (Petrus, a stone) (May 22). Christ is the chief cornerstone (May 23, title). In the author's classification the human race has **three groups: Jews, Gentiles, and the Church** (Oct 30).

**Translation notes.** Capitalise "Church" for the universal Body; keep "church" (lower case) for a local congregation if the source does. "Church is not a building" must be preserved — if the target-language word for "church" means "building", ask the translator how to render it.

**Sources.** July 13 *The Church Is More Than a Building*; Nov 22 *His Fullness*; May 22; Oct 30.

#### 4.24 Kingdom, citizenship, dominion and authority

**Meaning.** The believer is **not of this world**; he is a citizen of the Kingdom of God, "a nation without borders" with members in every nation, a **reigning champion** and **ambassador**, living by heaven's principles (Nov 24). **Legal position:** believers are raised and **seated together with Christ in heavenly places**, "far above all principality, and power…" — as the President's office does not change with his location, the believer's position does not change with circumstances (Nov 1). Hence **dominion** (reigning in life, Rom. 5:17) and **authority** are normal; **victory** is perpetual (2 Cor. 2:14; Nov 20).

**Translation notes.** "Dominion" and "reign" are positive, spiritual terms here — avoid political or oppressive connotations. Keep the President/office illustration or ask the translator for a culturally suitable parallel.

**Sources.** Nov 1 *Our Legal Position*; Nov 24; Nov 20 *Absolute Victory Every Day*; Nov 18; July 27.

### I. Spiritual warfare

#### 4.25 Satan, demons and spiritual warfare

**Meaning.** Satan has a **structured kingdom** (hierarchy and coordination) but is **a defeated foe**: Jesus "disarmed principalities and powers" (Col. 2:15). "Satan is not a factor" and is "under our feet" (Nov 14); the believer, seated with Christ, has authority over him (Mark 16:17, Matt. 10:8). Mark 3:27's "strong man" is Satan bound by Jesus (June 5); "demons are not a factor" (June 30, title). Believers do not live in fear of what Satan might do.

**Translation notes.** Keep the "defeated" and "under our feet" claims — do not soften to "limited". Avoid wording that suggests the devil is equal to God or that believers should fear him.

**Sources.** June 5 *A Defeated Kingdom*; Nov 14 *We Think Differently*; June 26–27 (titles: *Spiritual Weapons for Spiritual Warfare*; *The Reality of Spiritual Warfare*); Oct 21 (title: *The Strong Man Has Been Bound*).

#### 4.26 Holy Communion

**Meaning.** The author teaches that Holy Communion is "far more than a religious ritual or symbolic observance": a divine ordinance instituted by the Lord Jesus, carrying spiritual significance and power. He stresses that Jesus said "This **is** my body", not "this represents" it, and that Paul calls the cup and bread "the communion of the blood / of the body of Christ" (1 Cor. 10:16). Through the ministry of the Holy Spirit there is a **spiritual participation and fellowship** with Christ's body and blood. To **discern the Lord's body** (1 Cor. 11:29) means to recognise accurately, understand spiritually and respond with faith; failure to discern is linked to weakness and sickness (1 Cor. 11:30).

**Translation notes — HIGH SENSITIVITY.** Communion/Eucharist wording differs between traditions and Bible versions. HAN must **not** replace "is" with "represents" or "symbolises", must keep the verb "discern", and must flag the article for the doctrinal reviewer. Keep it distinct from *koinōnia* in 4.21 even though the English word "communion" is shared.

**Sources.** Sept 6 *More Than a Symbolic Observance*.

## 8. Quick lookup — one-line meanings for HAN

- **Righteousness** — God's own rightness given as a gift to the believer; received, not earned; does not grow.
- **Justification** — God's legal declaration "righteous / not guilty".
- **Holy Spirit** — a divine Person, God Himself, who lives in and is one with the believer's spirit.
- **Anointing** — the Holy Spirit in the believer; nothing "extra" to chase.
- **Word of God** — carries God's life; seed, food, manual, environment; also Jesus, the living Word.
- **Truth** — integrity; God's Word; the revelation of God's reality in Christ.
- **Grace** — God's favour/influence on a life; can increase; all-promoting.
- **Mercy** — already obtained; covenant kindness (*hesed*) not based on merit.
- **Wisdom** — Christ made wisdom to the believer; divine, discerning, indwelling.
- **Knowledge** — knowing God, who you are in Christ, and the Word — relational, not just information.
- **Consciousness** — awareness of settled realities in Christ (not "conscience").
- **New creation** — a believer born of God with His life and nature; traced to Christ, not ancestry.
- **Fellowship** — koinōnia: fellowship → partnership → distribution.
- **Faith** — expectation of God's Word's fulfilment and acting on it; founded on the Word, not feelings.
- **Offering** — holy act of worship/testimony; not a donation.
- **Holy Communion** — a divine ordinance with real spiritual participation, not mere symbol.
- **Church** — people, not a building; Christ's Body and His fullness.
- **Gospel** — God's power unto salvation; reveals righteousness; divine substitution.
- **Satan** — structured but defeated; under the believer's feet.
- **Compassion** — *no reference in sources reviewed*.
